Working With Horizon

Book Consultation

01

Initial Consultation

Every project begins with a conversation. We take the time to understand how you intend to use the space, what you want to achieve, and the opportunities and constraints surrounding your site. We will discuss your aspirations, budget, timeframe, and any specific requirements before outlining how we can approach the project together.

02

Design & Development

Our design process combines creative exploration with careful consideration of the practical requirements of your project. Initial concepts are developed and tested against the brief, site conditions, planning requirements, and budget. Through presentations and collaborative discussions, we refine the design into a clear architectural direction that reflects both the character of the project and the people who will use it.

03

Documentation & Delivery

A successful project depends on more than a strong concept. Our team develops detailed drawings and documentation while coordinating with engineers, consultants, contractors, and other project stakeholders. We work through the technical requirements of the design so that the architectural intent is clearly communicated and the project is prepared for the next stage.

04

Construction & Completion

Our involvement does not end when construction starts. We continue to provide architectural guidance, respond to queries, review progress, and help resolve issues as they arise. By remaining engaged throughout construction, we can help ensure the finished project reflects the decisions and design principles established throughout the process.
